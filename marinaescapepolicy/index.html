Marina Escape: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This policy explains how the mobile game Marina Escape: Boat Puzzle by Lamp Soft Nepal ("the game", "we") handles information. We built the game to need as little information as possible.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress (levels completed, stars, hints and sound settings) only on your own device. We do not have accounts, and this information is never sent to us. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Information we collect

We do not ask for your name, email, contacts, precise location, photos or any other personal data. The only data collected is the device information used by the ad services described below.

Advertising

The game is free and shows ads: an optional video you can choose to watch for extra hints, and an occasional full-screen ad between levels (never during play). Ads are provided by Unity LevelPlay and its ad network partner Unity Ads. To show, measure and personalise ads and to prevent fraud, these services collect device information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address (which can indicate approximate location), device model, operating system, language, network type and how you interact with ads, as described in the Unity privacy policy. We do not receive this information in a way that identifies you. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, at any time in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

We do not hold personal data ourselves. Data collected by the ad services is handled under their own privacy and security policies.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above.
